👨‍🍳Head chef at the pass = a session. Its line = the repo.
🧑‍🍳Line cook = a subagent; same apron colour as its chef, ticket on the rail = its lane.
🔪Chopping at the board = edit
🔥Pot on a lit burner, steam = run
🧾Holding up the ticket = read
🚪Crates from the blue walk-in = web
📣Arm up, tickets flying to stations = delegate
🥄Tasting from a bowl = think
🧽Wiping the counter = other
✨A flare of sparks over the burner = a new tool call just now
🔔Needs permission: red heat lamp, a plate up for your sign-off, bell ringing
❓Has a question: red heat lamp, bell ringing, the chef holds up a ticket with a red "?"
☕Leaning on the counter with a mug = resting cook
🧊Clean stations, burners off = idle
🌑Dark pass, hanging apron = offline
💨Pot boiling over, red smoke = stalled (a chef busy, or a cook quiet, for 5+ min)
📌Spike at the head of the line: spent tickets, taller the more tool calls the repo has seen
